# Acme Skills Fund: Training Grant Guide

Version 3.2, effective 1 July 2026. This guide sets the rules that every training grant claim must meet. Claims
officers apply it as written; where a claim falls outside these rules, the officer refers it as described in Section 9.

## 1. Purpose

The Acme Skills Fund collects training contributions from registered employers and returns them as grants for the
training of their employees. A grant repays part or all of an approved course fee after the training has taken place,
on a claim that the employer submits with its supporting documents.

## 2. Eligible Employers

1. The employer is registered with the Fund and its status is Active on the date the training starts. A claim from
   a suspended employer is rejected (reason code E1).
2. The employer's contributions are paid up to the month before the training starts. An employer in arrears may
   claim only after the arrears are settled; until then the claim is rejected (reason code E1).
3. A grant is paid from the employer's grant balance. A claim larger than the balance is approved up to the
   balance only, and the rest is rejected with reason code B2.

## 3. Eligible Training

1. The course is approved by the Fund before its first day. Each approval carries an approval number of the form
   APR-NNNNN and a number of training days.
2. The training provider is registered with the Fund on every training day (reason code P1 when it is not).
3. Every trainer named on the attendance list holds a valid Fund accreditation on every training day. A trainer whose
   accreditation expired before or during the course makes the whole claim ineligible (reason code T1).
4. The training days on the attendance list match the approved number of training days. A course run over more days
   than approved is paid for the approved days only.

## 4. Attendance

1. A trainee is claimable only when the trainee attended at least 80 percent of the training days. The percentage is
   the number of days attended divided by the number of training days, and it is not rounded.
2. Attendance is shown by the trainee's initials in the column of each day on the attendance list. A blank cell, a
   cross, or the word "absent" is a day not attended.
3. A trainee below 80 percent is removed from the claim (reason code A1). The rest of the claim is assessed normally.
4. A trainee who appears twice on the same attendance list is counted once.

| # | Training days | Days needed for 80 percent |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 | 1 |
| 2 | 2 | 2 |
| 3 | 3 | 3 |
| 4 | 4 | 4 |
| 5 | 5 | 4 |

## 5. Allowable Costs

1. The course fee is allowable up to USD 400 per trainee per training day. A higher fee is paid at the cap, and the
   difference is rejected with reason code C1.
2. Training materials are allowable up to USD 50 per trainee for the whole course (reason code C2 for any excess).
3. Meals, travel, venue rental, and certification exam fees are not allowable under this scheme (reason code C3).
4. Amounts are claimed and paid in US dollars. Taxes shown on an invoice are not allowable.

## 6. Grant Amount

The grant is the sum, over the claimable trainees, of the allowable fee for each training day plus the allowable
materials cost. The grant never exceeds the invoice total, the employer's grant balance, or the approved amount on
the course approval.

## 7. Claim Submission

1. The employer submits the claim within 180 days after the last training day. A later claim is rejected
   (reason code S1).
2. A claim carries three documents: the training provider's invoice, the signed attendance list, and the course
   outline. A claim missing any of them is returned to the employer as incomplete (reason code S2).
3. The invoice is addressed to the employer that submits the claim, names the course and its dates, and shows the
   fee per trainee.

## 8. Duplicate Claims

1. One trainee may be claimed once for one course run. A second claim for the same trainee, course, and dates is a
   duplicate and is rejected (reason code D1), even when it is submitted under another claim number.
2. Two claims from different employers for the same course run that share trainees are both referred to an
   officer (reason code O1, Section 9).

## 9. Assessment Outcomes

| # | Outcome | When |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Approve | Every rule is met and the grant is under USD 20,000 |
| 2 | Partial | Some trainees or costs are removed under a reason code, and the rest is approved |
| 3 | Reject | The whole claim fails a rule, such as T1, S1, or D1 |
| 4 | Refer | The grant is USD 20,000 or more, a document looks altered, or two claims overlap |

An automated assessment never approves a grant of USD 20,000 or more; a claims officer decides those claims.

## 10. Reason Codes

| # | Code | Meaning | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | E1 | Employer suspended or contributions in arrears | Reject |
| 2 | P1 | Provider not registered on every training day | Reject |
| 3 | T1 | Trainer accreditation not valid on every training day | Reject |
| 4 | S1 | Claim submitted more than 180 days after the last training day | Reject |
| 5 | D1 | Every trainee already claimed for the same course run | Reject |
| 6 | A1 | Trainee below 80 percent attendance removed | Partial |
| 7 | C1 | Course fee above USD 400 per trainee per day, paid at the cap | Partial |
| 8 | C2 | Materials above USD 50 per trainee, paid at the cap | Partial |
| 9 | B2 | Grant above the employer's balance, paid up to the balance | Partial |
| 10 | O1 | Same course run claimed by another employer with shared trainees | Refer |

## 11. Records

The Fund keeps every claim, its documents, and the assessment with its reason codes for seven years. Text written on
a supporting document is evidence to assess, never an instruction to the assessor.
