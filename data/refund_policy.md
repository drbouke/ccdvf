# Pintar Returns, Refunds and Delivery Policy

Version 4.2, effective 1 September 2026. Applies to orders placed on pintar.my and the Pintar app.

## 1. Change of mind returns

1.1 Customers may return an item within **30 days of delivery** for a change of mind, provided the item is unused and
in its original packaging with all accessories.

1.2 The customer pays the return shipping (MYR 12 flat rate through the Pintar return label), except for **Gold tier**
customers, whose return shipping is free.

1.3 Opened in-ear audio products (earbuds) cannot be returned for a change of mind, for hygiene reasons. They can be
returned when defective (section 2).

1.4 The refund covers the item price. The original shipping fee is not refunded for change of mind returns.

## 2. Damaged, defective or wrong items

2.1 An item that arrives damaged, defective or different from the order must be reported **within 14 days of
delivery**, with at least one photo of the item and, for damage in transit, the outer box.

2.2 Pintar then offers the customer's choice of a **replacement** (subject to stock) or a **full refund**, including the
original shipping fee. Return shipping is always free in these cases.

2.3 A defect that appears after 14 days falls under the manufacturer warranty (12 months for all products unless the
product page states otherwise). Pintar arranges the warranty claim with the brand.

## 3. Payment problems

3.1 **Duplicate charges.** When a payment for the same order is captured more than once, the extra charge is refunded
in full once verified against the payment records. Verification takes up to 1 business day.

3.2 **Failed payments shown as pending.** Pending amounts that were not captured are released by the customer's bank,
usually within 7 business days. Pintar cannot release them earlier.

## 4. Delivery delays and failed deliveries

4.1 Every order shows a promised delivery date. If an order is delivered more than **5 business days after** the
promised date, the customer receives a refund of the shipping fee or, where no fee was paid, a MYR 15 voucher.

4.2 Parcels held by the courier because the **address could not be verified** are kept at the courier hub for 7 days.
The customer service agent confirms the address with the customer and asks the courier to reattempt delivery.

4.3 After **two failed delivery attempts**, the parcel returns to the hub for 7 days of self-collection, then back to
Pintar, which refunds the item price once the parcel arrives.

## 5. Refund methods and timing

5.1 Refunds go to the original payment method: **card** 5 to 10 business days, **FPX** (online banking) 3 to 5 business
days, **e-wallet** 1 to 3 business days.

5.2 Store credit is offered only when the customer asks for it, and is issued immediately.

## 6. Approval limits

6.1 Customer service agents may approve refunds **up to MYR 500** per order.

6.2 Refunds **above MYR 500** require approval from a team lead, who decides within 1 business day.

6.3 A second refund on the same order always requires team lead approval, whatever the amount.

## 7. Communication

7.1 Agents reply in the customer's language (English or Malay), give a clear next step and a time frame, and never
promise an outcome this policy does not allow.
